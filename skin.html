Skin Preview

Render any Teeworlds skin with custom body and foot colors.

Loading skin…

How to use

Change the skin and colors with URL parameters:

  • skinname= the skin name, e.g. default.
  • color_body= integer Teeworlds body color.
  • color_feet= integer Teeworlds feet color.

Example:

https://zelamus.github.io/TeeViewer/skin.html?skinname=default&color_body=65535&color_feet=16711680
